# High Timeline — MVP product requirements

High Timeline is a private, device-only journal for recording the changing shape of a subjective cannabis session. Its central object is a timestamped curve: consumption, felt effects, intensity changes, contextual events, re-dosing, and the return to normal.

## Audience and promise

Adults of legal age who want a neutral record of their own experience. A state is always a self-report. The product never diagnoses, estimates sobriety, recommends dosage, or indicates whether driving is safe.

## Core journey

1. Complete a brief age, privacy, and safety introduction.
2. Start a session with a method and optional product details.
3. Record intensity changes in one tap, including repeated peaks.
4. Add effects, cannabis re-doses, nicotine, food, water, and other context without restarting.
5. End by self-reporting “Back to normal,” add an optional recap, and review derived durations.
6. Revisit, edit, export, share an anonymized local card, and inspect personal journal patterns.

## Product boundaries

No accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertisements, payments, location, community, medical guidance, impairment prediction, or external APIs. All records stay in IndexedDB unless the person explicitly downloads or shares them.

## Success criteria

The live experience is one-handed at 390 × 844, survives refresh, supports undo and safe editing, calculates multiple peaks correctly, works offline after first production load, and provides accessible history, insights, import/export, and privacy-safe sharing.
